# Random

**Flavour**: Clean, Psytrance, Forest, Darkpsy, Hi-Tech, Experimental. Pick a neighbourhood, then roll.

Buttons: **All**, **Safe**, **FX**, **Mutate**. They sit on the Voice strip next to Flavour. All is the full dice; Safe keeps more of the current idea; FX randomizes the rack; Mutate nudges what you already have.

If the result is terrible, that is information. Hit it again, or step back to a factory seed and pretend this never happened.
