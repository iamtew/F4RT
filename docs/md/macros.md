# Macros

Size, Wetness, Anger, Alienness, Squelch, Rip Length, Toxicity. They live on the Sound page under Character, and they bake into decay, resonance, FM, formant motion, and release.

Size and Rip are read when the note starts, so twisting them mid-toot will not rewrite the note that is already in the air. Comb is the filter type, not a Squelch threshold. Easy to mix up, once.

A few voice facts that belong here because they shape the same note:

- A tap — amp sustain near zero, including the Play button — finishes that one note even when note-off arrives in the same block. Amp sustain up is a normal held note.
- Glide is the slide time for mono and legato. Zero snaps. Poly ignores it.
- Bend is the pitch-bend range: 2, 12, or 24 semitones.
- Osc pan is stereo through the formant, filter, and distortion.
