# Matrix

![Matrix](matrix.png)

**Cables** — the first 16 of 32 sit here (On, From, To, Amount). A route does nothing until its **On** switch is lit, so you can wire things up without immediately modulating yourself into a puddle.

Right-click **Cutoff**, **Resonance**, **Morph**, or **Grit Drive** on Sound and pick LFO 1–4, velocity, aftertouch, a macro, or mod wheel. That writes the first free row here (and turns it On). Pick the same source again to switch that row Off. The six envelopes are not in that menu: they are already hardwired (see [Motion](motion.md)). Dest knobs grow a coloured arc and a source badge while a cable is live.

Pitch, Amp, Pan, and extra FM still want a row on this page.

The remaining slots exist as automatable parameters. They are just less fun to look at.

Source **Mod wheel** is MIDI CC1.
